## 中文

### 新功能
- 移动端游戏资源通过弹窗展示分享链接、提取码与解压密码，支持分别复制和一键复制全部。

### 改进
- 补齐阅读、下载、资料、音声字幕与弹幕等页面的文本翻译，已命中词包的内容直接使用译名，编号、链接、密码和账号保持原文。
- 屏蔽预设区分普通规则与扩大范围规则，支持分别编辑和删除，减少歧义词造成的误判。
- 次元茶馆与 Literotica Audio 站内使用文字列表，搜索与筛选结果保持一致；主页和域页继续使用卡片。
- 无封面作品使用手柄、耳机、书本等内容图标，退出账号时显示处理进度并防止重复点击。
- 播放支持原站返回的新 CDN 地址，减少站点更换播放域名或表单参数造成的失效。

### 修复
- EH 联想支持本地词库原词、译名及简繁输入匹配。
- 修复结构化标签缓存与详情屏蔽遗漏，统一屏蔽提示并补齐页面临时显隐入口。
- 详情顶部标题与副标题完整换行，避免长译文被行数限制截断。
- 修复次元茶馆回复获取分享链接时提示站点表单地址无效的问题。
- 修复部分站点表单、分页与跳转地址变化后无法继续浏览的问题。

## English

### New
- Mobile game resources now show sharing links, access codes, and archive passwords in a dialog, with individual and copy-all actions.

### Improvements
- Expanded text translation across reading, downloads, metadata, voice subtitles, and live comments. Dictionary matches use their translations directly, while IDs, links, passwords, and accounts remain unchanged.
- Blocking presets now separate standard and expanded rules, with independent editing and deletion to reduce ambiguous matches.
- Cycg and Literotica Audio now use text lists within the site, including search and filtered results; Home and domain pages retain cards.
- Works without covers use content icons such as gamepads, headphones, and books. Signing out shows progress and prevents repeated taps.
- Playback accepts new CDN addresses returned by sites, reducing failures when playback domains or form parameters change.

### Fixes
- EH suggestions now match local original names, translations, and simplified or traditional Chinese input.
- Fixed lost structured tags in cached lists and missing detail filtering, with consistent notices and temporary visibility controls.
- Detail titles and subtitles wrap fully, avoiding line limits that cut off longer translations.
- Fixed the invalid site form address error when replying to retrieve sharing links on Cycg.
- Fixed browsing failures caused by changes to site forms, pagination, and redirect addresses.
