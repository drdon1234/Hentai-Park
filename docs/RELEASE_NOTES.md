## 中文

### 新功能
- 多集音声支持可选拼接播放，统一音频进度与字幕时间轴。
- ASMR Gay、ASMR Moon 支持树状目录浏览，搜索结果也可展开目录。

### 修复
- 修复图片反搜结果未复用图片缓存、吃瓜历史封面缺失的问题。
- 修复重叠页和空页导致列表或音声目录提前停止加载的问题。
- 修复 ASMRHoney 合集无法打开的问题，支持嵌套合集及图集。
- 修复音声字幕高亮越界、目录展开状态恢复异常及快速切曲定位错误。
- 恢复黄豆、黄果和 91 短剧播放。
- 修复部分在线游戏跳转失败及重启应用后重复下载大型资源的问题。

### 插件更新
- ASMRHoney 0.0.3、ASMR Gay 0.0.3、ASMR Moon 0.0.3。
- 黄豆短剧 0.0.10、黄果短剧 0.0.13、91短剧 0.0.10。
- itch.io 0.0.3。

### 注意事项
- 拼接播放在切换曲目时可能短暂缓冲；在线游戏复用资源后仍可能需要解压和初始化。

## English

### New
- Added optional joined playback for multi-track audio works, with a unified playback and subtitle timeline.
- Added expandable directory browsing for ASMR Gay and ASMR Moon, including search results.

### Fixes
- Fixed image cache reuse in reverse image search and missing covers in article history.
- Fixed overlapping or empty pages prematurely stopping lists and audio directories.
- Fixed ASMRHoney collections, including nested collections and photo galleries.
- Fixed overflowing subtitle highlights, restored directory expansion states, and seeking during rapid track changes.
- Restored playback for Huangdou, Huangguo, and 91 short dramas.
- Fixed redirects for some online games and repeated downloads of large resources after restarting the app.

### Plugin updates
- ASMRHoney 0.0.3, ASMR Gay 0.0.3, ASMR Moon 0.0.3.
- Huangdou 0.0.10, Huangguo 0.0.13, 91 short dramas 0.0.10.
- itch.io 0.0.3.

### Notes
- Joined playback may briefly buffer between tracks; online games may still unpack and initialize cached resources.
